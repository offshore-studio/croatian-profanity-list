# Credits

## LDNOOBW V2 (Croatian)

This project incorporates terms from the Croatian wordlist in:

**[List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene and Otherwise Bad Words V2](https://github.com/LDNOOBWV2/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words_V2)**  
File: [`data/hr.txt`](https://github.com/LDNOOBWV2/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words_V2/blob/main/data/hr.txt)  
License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

We imported **148** lemmas/phrases that were not already covered by our curated
seeds (78 single words + 70 multi-word phrases). Clear false positives and
broken English calques were skipped; see
[`sources/ldnoobw-v2-import-report.txt`](sources/ldnoobw-v2-import-report.txt).

A vendored snapshot of the upstream `hr.txt` is kept at
[`sources/ldnoobw-v2-hr.txt`](sources/ldnoobw-v2-hr.txt) for reproducibility.

Thank you to the LDNOOBW / LDNOOBWV2 maintainers for publishing open
moderation wordlists.

## Local curation

Seed lemmas, inflection expansion, severity tagging, and search-oriented
exports were curated for South Slavic content moderation (Croatian-first,
with BA/RS/ME overlap).
